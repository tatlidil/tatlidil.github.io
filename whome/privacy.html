Who, Me? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who, Me? is a pass-the-phone word party game for iPhone. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Who, Me? does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The player names you type, your game settings, the session scores and a list of recently used words (so words do not repeat) are saved only on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Deleting your data

You can remove players on the players screen and reset scores on the results screen. Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
