Who, Me? — Support

The word party game where one of you is faking it.

How to play

Add 3–20 players and pass the phone around. Each player presses and holds the card to see the secret word, then lets go and passes the phone on. One player (or more) is the imposter and does not get the word. Going around the group, everyone says one word about their word. Then count to three, point at your suspect and tap the player with the most fingers pointing at them. The group wins when every imposter is caught; the imposters win if they survive or if a caught imposter guesses the word.

Modes

Hint: imposters know who they are and get the category plus one hint word. No hint: imposters only know they are the imposter. Undercover: imposters get a similar word and do not know they are the imposter. You can play with 1, 2 or 3 imposters.

Languages

English, 中文 (简体), हिन्दी, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português, Русский, Bahasa Indonesia and Türkçe. Each language has its own word list written for its speakers. Change the language with the globe button on the home screen.

Sound

If you hear nothing, check the silent switch and volume. Sound effects, music and vibration can be changed in Settings (the gear icon).
