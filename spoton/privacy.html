Spot On! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Spot On! is a family game of short precision challenges for iPhone. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Spot On! does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your personal bests, medals, Daily 5 results, streak, party player names and settings are saved only on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates a text and an image of your result and hands them to the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share with.

Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress deletes your saved progress. Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
