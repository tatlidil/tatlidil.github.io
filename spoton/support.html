How good is your eye?

How to play

Each challenge asks for one thing: draw a perfect circle, cut the cake exactly in half, pour right to the line, stop the watch at exactly the shown time, and more. Do it as precisely as you can. The game shows you the exact answer and scores you from 0 to 100%.

Modes

Daily 5: the same five challenges for everyone, once a day. Endless: keep scoring above the bar or lose one of three hearts. Party: 2–6 players pass one phone. Practice: replay any challenge and earn medals.

Sound

If you hear nothing, check the silent switch and volume. Music, sounds and haptics can be changed in Settings (the gear icon).
