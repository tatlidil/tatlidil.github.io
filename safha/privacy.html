Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

Safha collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

  • Bookmarks, highlights, notes, your last reading place and khatm progress.
  • Your settings (theme, translations, text size).

If iCloud is turned on for Safha, iOS syncs the items above through your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). We cannot see them.

Permissions

Safha asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications.

Children

Safha is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.
