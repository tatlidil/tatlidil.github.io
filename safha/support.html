Safha · صفحة

Support

Safha is a free, ad-free Quran reader: the 604-page Madinah mushaf, line for line, as a two-page book on iPhone Duo and iPad.

Turning pages

The mushaf is a right-to-left book: swipe from the left edge (or drag the left page) to go forward. Settings → Page turn switches between a page curl and a slide.

iPhone Duo

Unfold the phone and hold it like a book: Safha shows two facing pages with the crease as the spine. Tap an ayah to see its translation on the facing page. Held tall, unfolded, Safha shows one large page. Folded, one page fits the cover display.

Translations

Reading mode shows the Arabic with any of six translations. Tap the chips at the top to switch; your place stays. Long-press an ayah, or tap "All languages", to compare every translation.

Search

Type Arabic with or without harakat, any word in a translation, or a reference such as 2:255.

Sync

Bookmarks, highlights, notes and progress sync through your own iCloud when iCloud is on for Safha. There is no account.

Sources
