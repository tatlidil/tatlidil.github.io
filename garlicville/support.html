How to play

Add 4 to 20 players in the order you sit, choose the narrator (the phone or a person), the vote style and any extra roles, then deal the roles. Each player privately presses and holds their card to see their role. At night the vampires visit one player, who goes out of the game; special roles use their powers. By day everyone talks and votes to banish a suspect. The village wins when no vampire is left; the vampires win when they are as many as everyone else; the Jester wins alone if banished by vote. The full rules are in the app under "How to play".

Why does a night turn take 12 seconds?

When the phone narrates, every player's secret turn looks the same and lasts the same time, so nobody can tell who is a vampire by watching how long someone holds the phone.

I closed the app in the middle of a game

Open it again and tap "Continue game". The game carries on exactly where it was; a secret turn that was not finished starts again from the "Pass the phone" screen.
