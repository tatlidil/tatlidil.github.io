Garlicville — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Garlicville is a pass-the-phone vampire party game for iPhone. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Garlicville does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The player names you type, your settings and setup choices, the game in progress (so you can continue after closing the app) and last game's vampires (so the same person is less likely to be a vampire twice in a row) are saved only on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Deleting your data

You can remove players on the players screen and end a game from the game menu. Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
