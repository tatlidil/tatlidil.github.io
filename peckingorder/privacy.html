Pecking Order — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Pecking Order is a party game for one iPhone and a group of friends. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Pecking Order does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The names you type for your flock, your records, the quotes you save to the Book, the cards you write and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share at the end of a game, the app creates a short text and an image of your result and hands them to the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share with.

Deleting your data

Flocks & Book > a flock > Delete flock removes that flock. Settings > Erase all data deletes everything. Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game is made for teens and adults (13+) and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
