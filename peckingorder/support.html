Pecking Order — Support

Get a secret number. Say something that fits it. Line up the flock.

How to play

Every round shows a theme and a scale from 1 to 100, for example Superpowers: 1 = Useless, 100 = Godlike. Pass the phone around; each player holds the egg to see a secret number and lets go to hide it. Out loud, everyone names something from the theme that fits their number. Then drag the chickens into order, lowest at the bottom, and lock it in. The eggs crack from the bottom up.

Modes

Climb (2–10 players, co-op): three hearts, each chicken out of place costs one, a Perfect Roost gives one back. Hot Seat (3–10): a Judge lines everyone up alone and scores for reading the room. Head to Head (4–10): two teams take turns.

Common questions

The screen does not go dark during a round. That is on purpose: the phone sits in the middle of the table while you talk.

How is "out of place" counted? The game keeps the longest run of chickens that are already in order; everyone else is out of place.

Will cards repeat? Each flock remembers the cards it has played, so new cards come first. You can reset this in Flocks & Book.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
