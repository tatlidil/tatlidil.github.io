Folio

Privacy Policy · Two-Page Bible · effective November 9, 2026

Folio collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of ours.

What stays on your device

Your reading settings, the place you last read, and the bookmarks, highlights and notes you make are stored on your iPhone or iPad.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Folio mirrors your bookmarks, highlights, notes and last-read place to your own iCloud key-value storage so they appear on your other devices. This storage belongs to your Apple Account; the developer cannot see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for Folio in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Network

All Bible texts are inside the app. Folio makes no network requests. Links to the publishers' licence pages open in Safari only when you tap them.

Children

Folio is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: onurus96@gmail.com.

Folio is free and contains no ads or purchases. Bible texts from eBible.org; see the app’s About & Licenses screen.
