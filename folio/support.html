Two pages on iPhone Duo

Unfold your iPhone Duo. Folio opens to facing pages. Choose Book pages (the chapter flows across both pages; swipe to turn) or Parallel languages (one translation on each page; tap a verse to light up its twin). On iPad, turn the device to landscape. You can switch spreads off in Settings › Two pages when unfolded.

Languages

Tap a language at the top: WEB and KJV (English), Türkçe (YTC), 中文 (和合本), हिन्दी (IRV), Español (Reina-Valera 1909), العربية (Van Dyck). You stay on the same verse. Touch and hold any verse to read it in all seven Bibles.

Bookmarks, highlights, notes

Tap a verse and choose a colour, the bookmark or the note icon. Everything is in ••• › Bookmarks & Notes and syncs through your own iCloud. Export them from Settings.

Is the text exact?

Yes. Every translation is bundled exactly as published by eBible.org and checked verse by verse against the publisher’s own plain-text edition. In ••• › About & Licenses tap Verify texts now to repeat the SHA-256 check on your device.

Widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, choose Folio. Touch and hold the widget › Edit Widget to choose the translation.
