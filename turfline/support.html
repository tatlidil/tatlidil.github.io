Walk. Claim. Defend.

How does it work?

Tap Start conquest and go for a walk. Every hexagon you pass becomes yours. Walk in a loop and everything inside it is yours too. Walk through another player's hexagons to weaken them; a weak one (strength 1) is stolen. Walk your own hexagons again on another day to make them stronger (up to 5). Hexagons you don't visit slowly fade back to strength 1.

The three modes

Conquest is online, against everyone. Solo is your own private board and works fully offline. Trails only draws lines where you walked and never shares anything.

My walk didn't claim anything

  • Anything faster than a run (about 25 km/h) doesn't count, so cars, bikes and trains won't claim hexagons.
  • Very inaccurate GPS points (indoors, in tunnels) are ignored.
  • Inside your privacy zone nothing is claimed in Conquest.
  • Conquest needs a connection when the walk starts. If you lose signal later, the walk is saved and syncs when you're back online.
  • There is a daily limit of 2,500 changes per player to keep the game fair.

Day and Night

Tap the sun/moon button on the map to switch between Auto, Day and Night, or choose in Settings.

Google Maps, Strava, Google Earth

The in-app map is Apple Maps. You can export all your walks as GPX or KML (Trails > list > Export) and open them in Google My Maps, Google Earth, Strava and others.

New phone

Your player is stored in your iPhone's Keychain. Moving it to a new phone with an encrypted backup or Quick Start keeps it.

Play safe

Watch where you are going, stay on public paths, never enter private property and never play while driving.
