Dropamine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Dropamine is a falling-blocks puzzle game for iPhone. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Dropamine does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, level, trophies, jokers and settings are saved only on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
