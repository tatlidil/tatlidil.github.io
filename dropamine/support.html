How to play

Drag to move the piece, tap to rotate, drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop and flick up to hold. Clear lines back to back to build combos and trigger FEVER mode. Jokers can save a tough board: tap one to use it.

Too much going on?

Open Settings and lower the effects intensity to calm down shakes, flashes and particles. Sound, music and haptics can be switched off there too.
