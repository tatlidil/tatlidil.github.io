How to play

Each round a few lanterns glow as embers. Tap an ember to preview where its light will go, and tap it again to light it. Light travels in the direction the lantern points and lights the next lantern in its path, which passes it on. Lanterns add Glow or Mult to the round's fire; your score is Glow × Mult. After every spark the lit lanterns turn one step, so plan your next spark on the new board. Reach the goal to win the round.

The market

Between rounds, spend coins on new lanterns (pick a cell and a facing), charms (lasting bonuses, up to five) and rites (one-time edits like moving or turning a lantern).

Your progress

Progress is saved automatically and only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress. See the privacy policy.
