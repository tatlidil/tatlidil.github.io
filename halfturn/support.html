Halfturn: Support

Every shape survives a half turn.

How to play

Split the board into shapes, one for each star. Every shape must look the same after half a turn around its star, and must hold together in one piece. Touch a star, then tap or drag over tiles to give them to it; each tile brings its twin on the other side of the star. Tap a tile of the star you are holding to take it back.

Is every puzzle fair?

Yes. Every puzzle in the game has exactly one answer, and it can be reached by reasoning alone, without guessing. Each one was checked by a solver before it shipped.

The daily puzzle

Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same date. Monday is gentle; the week gets harder towards Sunday. Past days stay playable from the home screen.

My progress

Progress is stored only on your device. It is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.
