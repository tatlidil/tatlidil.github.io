Halfturn: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Halfturn is a logic puzzle game for iPhone. This page explains what data the game handles.

No data collected

Halfturn does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your solved puzzles, best times, boards in progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the game hands a short line of text (puzzle number, date and time) to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Deleting your data

Settings > Erase all progress deletes your saved progress. Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
